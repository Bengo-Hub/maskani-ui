## Rules for public-page photos

- Stock photos illustrate a feature, never a specific estate. An estate listing shows only that
  estate's own photos, or a plain tile with its initials when it has none.
- No photos of real people's faces, and no photos showing another company's brand.
- Add a row here for every new photo, with its source link.
